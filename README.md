🌍 CrowdSolve AI
Real Problems. Crowd-Powered Solutions.

CrowdSolve AI is a community-driven platform where people can report real-world problems and work together to find practical solutions.

Instead of letting complaints disappear, CrowdSolve turns problems into collaborative challenges where the community can discuss, vote, propose solutions, and track progress.
